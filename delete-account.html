Account Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal information. If you no longer want to use PromptHub, you can request the deletion of your account and associated data.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete the personal information associated with your PromptHub account, including account information and cloud-stored prompts where applicable.

Some information may be retained when required by law or when necessary for legitimate security, fraud prevention, or legal purposes.

Important: Account deletion may be permanent. Once your account and associated data have been deleted, it may not be possible to recover your prompts or other account information.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion by contacting us using the email address below.

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your PromptHub account.
  2. Use "Account Deletion Request" as the email subject.
  3. Include the email address associated with your PromptHub account.
  4. We will process your request and take the appropriate steps to delete your account and associated data.
