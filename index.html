Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 4 October 2026

PromptHub ("we", "our", "us", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the PromptHub mobile application.

PromptHub is an application designed to help users save, organize, search, edit, and manage AI prompts.

Your privacy matters. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, PromptHub may handle the following information.

Prompts and Content

When you create or save prompts in PromptHub, the content you provide may be stored within the app or, where cloud synchronization is enabled, on our cloud services.

This may include:

  • Prompt titles
  • Prompt text
  • Categories
  • Tags
  • Favorites
  • Collections
  • Other information you choose to add to your prompts

Please do not include sensitive personal information in prompts unless you are comfortable storing that information within the app.

Account Information

If PromptHub provides account or cloud-sync functionality, we may collect information necessary to create and maintain your account, such as your email address and account identifier.

If you use PromptHub without an account, we do not require you to provide account information solely to use the basic features of the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by PromptHub may be used to:

  • Provide the app's features
  • Save and organize your prompts
  • Synchronize prompts between supported devices
  • Allow you to search and manage saved content
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Provide customer support
  • Protect the application from misuse, fraud, or security threats

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your prompts and other information may be stored locally on your device.

If PromptHub offers cloud backup or synchronization, information may also be stored on secure third-party infrastructure used to provide those services.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Third-Party Services

PromptHub may use trusted third-party services to provide functions such as:

  • Cloud storage
  • Authentication
  • Analytics
  • Crash reporting
  • Application infrastructure

These service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

If third-party services are added to PromptHub in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

5. Advertising

At this time, PromptHub does not intentionally sell your personal information to advertisers.

If advertising is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant advertising technologies and information practices.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

PromptHub may use analytics or crash-reporting services to understand application performance, identify technical problems, and improve reliability.

Such services may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Crash information
  • General technical diagnostics

We do not use crash reporting or analytics to intentionally collect the content of your private prompts.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve PromptHub.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of PromptHub, our users, or others.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may be able to:

  • Edit or delete your saved prompts
  • Remove collections and tags
  • Delete your account, where account functionality is provided
  • Stop using cloud synchronization
  • Uninstall PromptHub from your device

If PromptHub provides an account deletion feature, deleting your account will initiate deletion of information associated with that account, subject to information that we may be required to retain for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes.

For data deletion requests that cannot be completed directly within the application, users may contact us using the contact information below.

9. Children's Privacy

PromptHub is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by PromptHub.

However, no method of electronic storage, processing, or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Effective Date" will be updated.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us.
